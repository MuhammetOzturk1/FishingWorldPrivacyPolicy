Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Fishing World handles information on Android and iOS. The developer responsible for the game is Muhammed Ali Öztürk. For privacy questions, contact destek.bloke@gmail.com.

1. Game data and support

Fishing World does not require a player account. Game progress, gold, inventory, upgrades, quests, cooking timers, tutorial progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives these game saves. Your operating system may include app data in device backups depending on your settings.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

2. Advertising and consent services

The game integrates Google AdMob to offer rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy choices. Watching an eligible rewarded advertisement can provide the in-game reward shown on the offer.

Google's advertising SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them; device or app identifiers; ad views, taps and other interaction data; and diagnostic or performance information. The precise processing depends on the platform, SDK configuration, applicable consent and device settings. These data support ad delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Advertising services may communicate with their servers when initialized or when an ad is loaded, before you choose to watch it.

UMP processes consent-related information and stores privacy choices. Where required, the game presents privacy choices before requesting ads. The installed game does not contain a separate Firebase Analytics integration.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites.

3. Your privacy choices

When available for your region and consent status, open the game's settings and select AD PRIVACY to review or change advertising privacy choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and app tracking permissions through your device settings. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related processing.

You can remove local progress by clearing the app's data using supported device controls or by deleting the app and its data. Removing local data does not automatically remove copies in operating-system backups or information already processed by advertising providers. Backup controls are provided by your operating system.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, request portability, or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. Contact us to exercise applicable rights concerning information we hold. We may need proportionate information to verify your request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. For information held by Google, its privacy policy explains available controls and request channels.

4. Younger users

Fishing World is designed for teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed to children under 13. This describes our intended audience; it is not an age-verification mechanism or a guarantee that younger users cannot access the game. The game does not currently ask players to provide their age.

We do not request a name, email address or precise location to play. Advertising-related processing is described above; we do not claim that no information can be processed when a younger person uses the game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, contact us at destek.bloke@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion of information we hold where applicable. Requirements and protections for minors may differ by country and may also apply to teenagers.

5. Why information is processed and who receives it

Local saves enable gameplay and preserve preferences. Support correspondence is processed to answer requests and maintain support records. Advertising information is processed by Google and relevant advertising providers for the purposes described above. Information you email us is also handled by our email service provider. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect legitimate rights and security.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on consent for relevant advertising activities, providing a requested service, legitimate interests in support and security, or compliance with legal obligations, as applicable. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

6. Retention, transfers and security

Local saves remain until removed or replaced. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address the request and satisfy applicable legal obligations. Google determines retention for information it processes under its policies. Service providers may process information outside your country, including in countries with different privacy laws; their policies describe applicable transfer safeguards.

We take reasonable steps to protect information we control, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Avoid sharing sensitive information in support messages.

7. This policy website

This page is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no developer-added analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or cookies. GitHub may process technical visitor information to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are governed by the destination service's policies.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date at the top will identify the latest version. Where required, material changes will be communicated or new consent requested.
